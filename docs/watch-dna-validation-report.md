## Two-metric separation

- **Quiz Progress** measures onboarding position only (answered / 20). A starter
  checkpoint fires at 10; onboarding ends at 20.
- **Watch DNA Confidence** measures understanding, from 13 diverse signal types (raw weight
  total 133 before the 100 cap). Calibration answers alone saturate around
  ~60% — they can never reach 100% without breadth of engagement, so the two numbers never
  mirror each other.
